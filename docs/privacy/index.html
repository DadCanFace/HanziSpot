Hanzi Spot Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy describes how Hanzi Spot, an Android game published by DadCanFace, handles data.

Data the game uses

Hanzi Spot uses your answers to run the game and saves your best score for each difficulty in private app storage on your device. The game works offline. It has no accounts, advertising, or analytics, and it does not send your scores or gameplay data to the developer or third parties.

Pronunciation

When you choose to hear a character, the app passes that character to your Android text-to-speech engine using an installed offline voice. Hanzi Spot does not record microphone audio.

Storage, backup, and deletion

High scores remain on your device until you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. Depending on your Android backup settings, the operating system may back up and restore those scores. You can manage those backups in your device or account settings. DadCanFace does not hold a server copy of your scores.

Website and privacy questions

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes requests to deliver the website under its privacy statement.

For questions about this policy, contact DadCanFace through the public GitHub issue tracker. Please keep personal information out of public posts.
